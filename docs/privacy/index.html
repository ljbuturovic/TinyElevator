TinyElevator Privacy Policy

TinyElevator does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or analyze personal data.

Files and photos added to TinyElevator are copied into the app's private local storage on the user's device. TinyElevator does not operate a server or cloud service and does not transmit those files off the device.

TinyElevator does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data-collection services.
